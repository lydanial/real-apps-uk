Really Rude Reminders - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Really Rude Reminders is a reminder and calendar application developed by Real Apps UK. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal information under your control.

1. Information You Enter

The app allows you to create reminders containing information such as titles, dates, times, categories and recurring schedules.

Reminder information is stored locally on your device for the app to function. You do not need to create an online account to use the current version.

Real Apps UK does not operate a server that receives or stores your reminder entries in the current version.

2. Notifications

The app uses device notifications to deliver reminders at the times you configure.

Notification permissions may be requested. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

Reminder details may appear in notifications on your device, depending on your notification and lock-screen settings.

3. Permissions

The app may request permissions needed to deliver scheduled notifications and operate reliably.

These permissions are used for reminder functionality, not for accessing your personal files, contacts or messages.

4. Advertising and Purchases

Really Rude Reminders uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Test advertisements may be displayed during development and closed testing.

Google AdMob may collect and share the following information:

  • IP address and approximate location.
  • App interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Where required, users are presented with advertising privacy and consent choices.

Personal reminder details, including medication reminders, are stored locally and are not provided to advertising partners by the app.

The app offers an optional one-time Pro purchase to remove advertisements. Purchases are processed through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not directly collect or store payment card details.

5. Backup and Cloud Storage

The current version does not provide Google Drive backup or automatic cloud synchronisation.

Backup and restoration functionality may be introduced in a future update. Any associated account access, data handling and storage arrangements will be explained when implemented.

6. Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through platform services such as Google Play and Apple's App Store. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Any additional third-party services used by future versions will be disclosed in an updated privacy policy where applicable.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can edit or delete reminders within the app.

Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to any backup or restoration features provided by your device operating system.

8. Children's Privacy

The app contains an optional adult-humour mode featuring strong language and mature jokes. It is not designed for children.

9. Security

We aim to minimise the personal information required to operate the app. You should protect access to your device, particularly if your reminders contain sensitive information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features, permissions or data-processing practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
