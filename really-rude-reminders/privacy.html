Really Rude Reminders - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Really Rude Reminders is a reminder and calendar application developed by Real Apps UK. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal information under your control.

1. Information You Enter

The app allows you to create reminders containing information such as titles, dates, times, categories and recurring schedules, including medication reminders.

Reminder information is stored locally on your device for the app to function. You do not need to create an online account to use the app.

Real Apps UK does not operate a server that receives or stores your reminder entries in the current version.

2. Notifications

The app uses device notifications to deliver reminders at the times you configure.

Notification permissions may be requested. You can manage these permissions through your device settings.

Reminder details may appear in notifications on your device, depending on your notification and lock-screen settings.

Medication reminders can optionally be hidden from the calendar and upcoming events. This visibility setting does not disable their scheduled notifications.

3. Permissions

The app may request permissions needed to deliver scheduled notifications and operate reliably.

Reminder permissions are used for reminder functionality, not for accessing your personal files, contacts or messages.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may access advertising identifiers where permitted by your device settings and applicable privacy requirements.

4. Advertising and Purchases

Really Rude Reminders uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. Test advertisements may be displayed during development and closed testing.

Google AdMob may collect and share the following information:

  • IP address and approximate location.
  • App interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security and compliance. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Where required, users are presented with advertising privacy and consent choices.

Users can manage, reset or delete their advertising identifier through their device settings, where supported. Advertising privacy choices may affect the types of advertisements displayed and how advertising data is processed.

Personal reminder details, including medication information, are stored locally and are not provided to advertising partners by the app.

The app offers an optional one-time Pro purchase to remove advertisements. This is not a subscription.

Purchases are processed through Google Play or, when available on iOS, the Apple App Store. We do not directly collect or store payment card details.

For further information about Google's data handling practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google advertising privacy controls

5. Backup and Cloud Storage

The current version does not provide Google Drive backup or automatic cloud synchronisation.

Backup and restoration functionality may be introduced in a future update. Any associated account access, data handling and storage arrangements will be explained when implemented.

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party platform services, including Google Play and Google AdMob, and may use Apple's App Store when available on iOS.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

When you choose to share the app, leave a review or send feedback, you may be directed to an external application or service. Information you voluntarily provide through those services is subject to their applicable privacy policies.

If you contact Real Apps UK directly, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your correspondence. This information is used to respond to your enquiry or provide support.

Any additional third-party services used by future versions will be disclosed in an updated privacy policy where applicable.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can edit or delete reminders within the app, including medication reminders that have been hidden from the calendar.

Locally stored reminder information is retained until you delete it or remove the app's stored data.

Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored application data, subject to any backup or restoration features provided by your device operating system.

Advertising data processed by Google is subject to Google's own data retention and deletion policies. Deleting reminders or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete information previously collected by third-party advertising services.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information held directly by Real Apps UK, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

Really Rude Reminders contains adult humour, strong language and mature jokes. The app is intended for an adult audience and is not designed for children.

9. Security

We aim to minimise the personal information required to operate the app.

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

You should protect access to your device, particularly if your reminders contain sensitive or personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features, permissions or data-processing practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
