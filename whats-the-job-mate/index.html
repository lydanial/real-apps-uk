What's the Job, Mate?

Proper tool, for the job.

Built around real-world requirements. What's the Job, Mate? has grown out of years of conversations with tradespeople, garages and small businesses about quoting work — what they need, what gets in the way, and what existing ways of doing it don't make easy enough.

We were told what was needed. So we built it.

Now we want to put it into people's hands and find out where we got it right, where we missed, and what needs changing.

How do you really price a job?

We're running a quick anonymous survey for UK tradespeople about how jobs are actually priced — materials, markup, profit, quiet days, negotiating and those inevitable "Is that your best price?" conversations.

No name, business name or email address is required. It takes about 2 minutes.

Take the anonymous survey

Fancy trying it?

We're putting together our first group of UK tradespeople to test What's the Job, Mate? on Android.

The closed beta is currently going through Google's review process. Once we're given the green light, we'll invite our testers in to price a few jobs, have a proper play with it and tell us what's good, what's confusing, what's broken — or what's just plain shit.
