Privacy Policy

What's the Job, Mate?

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how What's the Job, Mate? (WTJM), provided by Real Apps UK, handles information when you use the app. For questions about this policy, please use the contact details below.

Information you enter into the app

WTJM lets you enter and save business and customer information for quoting, invoicing and managing work. This may include names, business contact details, addresses, descriptions of jobs, labour and material costs, prices, deposits, tax information, payment details, notes and business logos.

Job information is stored on your device to provide the features you choose to use. Real Apps UK does not operate a central database of your customers, jobs, quotes or invoices, and does not routinely receive that content. If you enable optional Google Drive backup, the information described below is also transferred to your own Google Drive account.

You are responsible for entering customer information appropriately and for deciding which details to include in documents you issue.

Quotes, invoices and PDF documents

WTJM creates quote and invoice PDFs using the details you provide. These may include names, addresses, job descriptions, amounts, tax details, bank or payment information and other business information. Issued PDFs and relevant job records can be retained on your device.

You control whether and how to share, save, print or send these documents outside WTJM. If you enable Google Drive backup, supported issued PDFs may also be included in that backup.

Optional Google Drive backup (Pro)

Pro users can choose to connect a Google account to back up and restore WTJM information. With your authorisation, WTJM uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API to store backups in your Google Drive's hidden app-data area. Backups can include saved jobs, relevant settings, issued quote and invoice PDFs, and your selected business logo. Your connected Google account email address and backup status information may be stored locally on your device.

This is an optional feature. Real Apps UK does not run a separate WTJM cloud-backup server. Backups are transferred to and stored by Google under the protections and terms of your Google account. WTJM does not add end-to-end encryption to the backup contents. The Google Drive app-data area is not normally visible among your ordinary Drive files, but data may remain in your Google account until you remove it.

You can disconnect your Google account from WTJM and manage or remove the app's stored data through your Google account and Drive settings. Disconnecting the app does not necessarily delete an existing Google Drive backup.

Google Play purchases and subscriptions

WTJM may offer a Pro subscription and optional purchases of PDF credits or other in-app upgrades. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment methods and payment processing; WTJM does not receive your full card details.

The app may process and store purchase status, product identifiers and transaction or purchase identifiers needed to provide and restore purchased features and avoid crediting a purchase twice. Google may process additional information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Rewarded advertising and your choices

The Free version of WTJM offers optional rewarded adverts, including as a way to unlock certain PDF actions without buying credits. Advertising is provided using Google AdMob. You choose whether to request and watch a rewarded advert; an alternative paid-credit option is available for supported actions.

Google's advertising technology may collect or process information such as your device's IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions and advert views, diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID where available. This information may be used for advert delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalisation. Google's own privacy terms govern its processing of this data.

Where required, WTJM uses Google's consent-management system to ask for your advertising privacy choices. You may be offered options to consent, decline or manage your choices. When applicable, you can revisit these choices using the advertising privacy option in the app. Declining consent may affect whether a rewarded advert is available; it does not prevent you from using the app's non-advertising features.

For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Technical information and other services

Google Play, Google AdMob, Google Sign-In, Google Drive and services provided by your device or operating system may handle technical information needed to run their features. Depending on the service, this can include device or app identifiers, diagnostic information, app interactions and account or purchase information.

Real Apps UK does not use the job and customer details you enter to build an advertising profile. We may receive information that you choose to send us when contacting support, such as your email address and the details of your enquiry, in order to respond.

Why information is used

WTJM uses information you enter to provide the quoting, invoicing, job-management and document features you request. Optional Google Drive access is used for backup and restore when you connect your account. Purchase information is used to manage paid features. Advertising information is processed by Google and its advertising partners for the purposes described above, subject to applicable privacy choices and legal requirements.

Data retention, deletion and your rights

You can manage and delete saved jobs through WTJM where that option is available. Uninstalling WTJM or clearing its app data may remove locally stored records and documents. Keep copies of important invoices and quotes before deleting app data or changing devices.

Removing local app data does not automatically remove PDFs you have shared elsewhere, Google Play purchase records or an existing Google Drive backup. You can manage Google-held information through your Google account and its privacy controls. Google and other service providers may retain information under their own policies and legal obligations.

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact us using the details below about information handled by Real Apps UK. For information held by Google, please use Google's privacy controls or contact Google.

Children's privacy

WTJM is a business productivity app intended for tradespeople and other small businesses. It is not designed for children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app or the services it uses change. The latest version will appear on this page, with a revised "Last updated" date.
