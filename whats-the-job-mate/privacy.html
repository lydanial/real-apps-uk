Privacy Policy

What's the Job, Mate?

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how What's the Job, Mate?, provided by Real Apps UK, handles information when you use the app.

Information you enter into the app

What's the Job, Mate? allows you to enter information such as job names, customer names, job costs, labour costs, pricing information, notes and other information used to prepare and save quotes.

The current version of the app stores this information locally on your device. Real Apps UK does not receive or centrally store the contents of your saved jobs or quotes.

PDF quotes

The app can create PDF quotes using information you have entered. PDFs are generated for your use and may contain information such as your business details, customer details, job descriptions and quote values.

You are responsible for deciding where those PDFs are saved, shared or sent after they are created.

Accounts and cloud storage

The current version of What's the Job, Mate? does not require you to create a Real Apps UK account and does not provide Real Apps UK cloud storage for your quotes or saved jobs.

Technical information

The app itself does not intentionally collect personal information for Real Apps UK. However, the platform through which the app is distributed, and services provided by your device or operating system, may process limited technical information such as device information, app performance information, crash information or diagnostics.

Such information is handled according to the privacy policies and settings of the relevant platform or service provider.

Advertising

The current beta version of What's the Job, Mate? does not contain third-party advertising.

If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what advertising services are used and what information those services may process.

How your information is used

Information you enter into the current version of the app is used on your device to provide the app's quoting, pricing, saving and PDF features.

Data retention and deletion

Because saved job and quote information is stored locally on your device, you can remove information by deleting saved jobs within the app where that option is available.

Removing the app or clearing its application data may also remove locally stored information. You should keep copies of any quotes or information you need before deleting app data or uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

What's the Job, Mate? is a business and productivity tool intended for tradespeople and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app changes, including if new features, advertising, cloud services or other third-party services are introduced.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed when appropriate.
