Privacy Policy

Where’s Your Bin, Mate? — Real Apps UK · Effective date: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Real Apps UK handles information in Where’s Your Bin, Mate? (the “app”), including advertising and optional in-app purchases.

1. Information you enter in the app

You may enter a name or nickname, bin names and colours, collection dates, reminder times, holiday changes, notification choices and your preferred Scruffy personality. The app saves these details locally on your device so reminders and stories work.

Real Apps UK does not operate user accounts for this app, and the app does not send your entered bin schedules or nickname to a Real Apps UK account or server as part of its normal operation. Device backups, if you enable them through your operating system, are controlled by your device provider.

2. Notifications

If you grant permission, the app schedules local bin-reminder notifications. These are generated on your device from your settings. You can change reminders in the app and disable notification permission in your phone’s settings.

3. Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device and app information, ad interactions and location-related signals to deliver, measure and protect adverts, subject to applicable law and your privacy choices. The app does not request your device’s precise GPS-location permission for the bin reminder feature.

Where required, an advertising-consent form lets you accept, decline or manage available options. You can revisit your advertising choices from the app’s Settings when that option is available. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily mean no advertisements will be displayed.

For more information about Google’s processing, read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s guidance on how it uses information from apps.

4. Optional payment — Remove Ads Forever

The app offers an optional one-off purchase to remove adverts. Google Play (or the relevant app store on other platforms) handles payment and transaction processing. The app receives purchase status and keeps an on-device record that advertising has been removed. Real Apps UK does not receive your full card details through the app. The store may retain transaction records under its own legal obligations and policies.

5. Who receives information

Real Apps UK does not intentionally sell your manually entered bin schedules or nickname. The advertising SDK and the relevant app store may process information under their own policies for advertising, consent management, purchases, security, fraud prevention and legal compliance. Information is not sent to a Real Apps UK-operated account because this app has no account system.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

Your manually entered app information remains in the app’s local storage until you delete it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall it, subject to any device backup/restore settings. You can delete individual bins in the app. To remove all locally stored app information, clear the app’s storage through your phone settings or uninstall the app. Advertising and app-store providers independently determine the retention of information they process; consult their policies and privacy controls.

7. Security

We design the app to keep your manually entered bin data on your device and use the Google-supported advertising, consent and purchase services. No online service or device can be guaranteed completely secure. Please keep your operating system updated and protect your device with a screen lock.

8. Children and language

Where’s Your Bin, Mate? includes an optional Bad Boy personality with strong language. It is not specifically directed at children. Please consult the age rating and content information displayed in the app store.

9. Your choices and questions

You can edit or remove bin details, switch notification settings, choose your personality mode, and manage advertising privacy choices where available. For questions about data handled by Real Apps UK, email chris@lydanialfacilities.co.uk. Requests about Google or app-store information may also need to be made directly to those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app, advertising services or legal requirements. The latest version will be available at this web address, with its effective date updated.
